What a first order looks like

Most first orders start with a sample, move to a mixed small run, then repeat. The terms below are the ones we quote as standard.

  • Sample first — 7 to 10 days, and the fee comes back as part of the credit once the order passes USD 8,000.
  • Start at 60 to 100 pieces for one design, and you can mix designs in the same run.
  • Bulk from 500 pieces gets a better unit price, with 25 to 55 days of production depending on quantity and finish.
  • Paid 30% up front by T/T, balance against the bill of lading copy, shipped FOB Ningbo or Shanghai.

Common questions about Yuzhou Crystal

How long has the factory been making crystal?
Since 2001, in Pujiang, Zhejiang — the main crystal craft cluster in China. The same team now runs 3,500 m² of production floor with 65 people and roughly 50,000 pieces a month.
Is your crystal free of lead?
Yes. Most pieces are cut from K9 optical crystal and every piece we ship is lead-free, with SGS and RoHS documentation available.
What does a buyer normally ask for first?
A sample with the final engraving, then a first run at the 60 to 100 piece level so the range can be tested. From 500 pieces the price per piece improves.
