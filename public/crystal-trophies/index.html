What to fix before a trophy order

Three things decide a trophy order before anyone talks about decoration: how the cup meets the base, how many heights you run, and where the engraving can physically sit.

Cup, stem and base as one or three parts

A three-piece trophy arrives as a cup, a stem and a base and screws together at the neck. Splitting the parts lets one cup body sit on different bases, so a single mould can cover several award tiers and price bands. A one-piece body reads heavier and stacks better, and is usually quoted when the programme has one fixed look.

Height steps that match the award

Most programmes run multiple height steps off a single silhouette, roughly 10 to 32 cm, so the champion piece simply stands taller than the runner-up. Clients order the whole ladder for one event and re-order only the top step the following year.

Where the engraving can actually sit

The curve of a cup takes a short line only; long titles and year after year name lists belong on the flat base. We place the engraving where you specify — on the cup, the stem or the base — based on your artwork and how the piece will be read.

Seasonal order windows

School and sports buyers place between March and August for the peak season and again in November for end-of-year ceremonies. A plain trophy ships in about 7 days; a fully custom order with engraving and proofing runs 7 to 20 days.

How crystal trophies and awards are made

The same four stages carry every order, from one award to a full container of cup parts. Seeing them once is usually enough to set a date.

Material selection

The run starts with the block. Clarity, inclusions and the optical tone are chosen before cutting, because the material decides how the facets will read once polished.

Blanking the cup profile

A block is sawn to a blank and roughed out along the cup and handle profile, then the facet lines are cut down onto the body.

Bringing the faces up

The cup is walked through the grades, coarse to final, which is what gives a mirror shoulder instead of a grey one. The rim is chamfered here so it will not flake in handling.

Setting the mark and the base

Laser, sandblast or etch work is laid over the approved text, the piece is held against the approved sample, then set onto a base and boxed for export.

What you can customise on trophies

The list below is what buyers most often specify. Anything on it is quoted per unit, so the clearer your brief at the start, the faster the quotation.

  • Silhouette — cup, beacon, block, shield, ionic, custom 3D form or an adapted existing mould
  • Material — optical crystal, glass or a heavier cast body for a premium weight in the hand
  • Size and thickness — proportions matched to the engraving area you need
  • Base — wood, marble, metal, acrylic or a two-part base with a coloured inlay
  • Engraving — laser, sandblast, etch-fill or UV print, in gold, silver, rose or black
  • Frosting and colour — clear, frosted, coloured gradient or activated edge light
  • Presentation — gift box, velvet pouch, ribbon and a private-label insert

Frequently asked questions about crystal trophies and awards

Can we put our own logo and text on the trophy?
Yes. Send your logo in a vector or high-resolution file and we will place it, size it and send a visual proof before anything goes into production. Text, names, dates and event branding are set to your layout.
Will the engraving survive shipping and handling?
Laser and sandblast engraving become part of the crystal surface rather than sitting on top of it, so they do not rub off. Each piece is packed individually in foam or a moulded insert inside a rigid box.
Do you make a sample before the full order?
Yes. We can produce a sample from your approved artwork so you can check the weight, the optical clarity and the engraving depth in your own hands. Sample cost and timing are confirmed before you approve.
What artwork files do you need from us?
Vector files (AI, EPS, PDF or CDR) with outlines for text are best for laser work. If you only have a raster logo, send it at 300 dpi or higher and our art team can prepare the file for engraving.
Can different sizes and models ship in one order?
Yes. Mixed designs, sizes and engraving lines can usually share one production run and one shipment, which keeps the unit cost down compared with separate orders. Tell us the breakdown and we will quote it together.
Do you work with our own packaging supplier?
Both. We can supply the finished trophy in our own export-ready box, or produce the crystal components body-only for assembly into packaging you provide under your own brand.

Still deciding? Send us the design, the quantity and the date you need it by, and we will come back with a quotation and a production plan.
