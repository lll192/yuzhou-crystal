Getting a candle holder order right

Before a holder is quoted it is worth fixing the candle format, the siting and the burn profile, because those three decide the body shape more than any decoration you might add later.

Candle format and the seat it needs

A tealight sits in a wide shallow bowl where the rim carries the heat rather than the base; a taper needs a narrow tubing seat with a visible collar to hide the join; a pill candle only asks for a shallow dish. Sizing the seat to the candle is what stops the wick bending over as the candle burns down.

Arm count, span and how it travels

Multi-arm candelabra are quoted by arm count and overall span. Both split-frame and one-piece candelabra are available. A split-frame arrives as separate arms and a column and is assembled on site, which keeps the carton small and the breakage low; a one-piece frame reads heavier on the table but needs a deeper box.

What the flame does with the surface

A mirror face throws the flame back as a second point of light; a frosted face softens it into a halo; a fluted column breaks it into a ring of bright lines. Most hospitality programmes order one mirror body and one satin body so the same table reads differently between lunch and dinner service.

Heat, crazing and upkeep

Crystal takes a thermal shock badly. A holder left standing on a hot tray, or moved straight from a warm room to a cold one, can craze along the rim. Buyers who run a permanent table keep a rotation of cleaned holders off service so the lit colony is always a known, even set.

How crystal candle holders are made

The same four stages carry every order, from a single candelabra to a full container of holder parts. Seeing them once is usually enough to book the run.

Material selection

The run starts with the block. Clarity, inclusions and the optical tone are chosen before cutting, because the material decides how the facets will read once polished.

Sawing and profiling

The block is sawn to a rough blank, then profiled along the arm line on cnc and conventional equipment before the faceted shoulders are brought down to size.

Polishing and edge work

Every face is brought up in sequence - coarse through to final - which is what separates a mirror shoulder from a hazy one. The bead at the outer edge is worked last to blunt the lip against chipping.

Marking and packing

Laser, sandblast or etch work is laid over the approved artwork, the piece is held against the approved sample, then laid into a shaped insert and boxed for export.

What you can customise on candle holders

The list below is what buyers most often specify. Anything on it is quoted per unit, so the clearer your brief at the start, the faster the quotation.

  • Candle fit — taper, pill, tealight, jar or a wide-bowl for a multi-stem arrangement
  • Height and proportion — a repeated height across a set for a uniform table
  • Holder construction — solid base, footed column, faceted bowl or a wall-mounted sconce
  • Facet and finish — mirror, frosted, fluted or a mixed satin and mirror body
  • Flame-safe base — a recessed seat and a heat-tolerant foot for long burns
  • Colour — clear, smoked, amber or a coloured gradient through the column
  • Packaging — gift box, branded sleeve or a hotel amenity carton

Frequently asked questions about crystal candle holders

Will the holder be stable with a lit candle?
We size the base and the seat to the candle diameter you use, so the holder stays steady for a full burn rather than tipping as wax is consumed.
Can I mix heights on one table?
Yes, and it is the usual approach — we supply a set in two or three heights from the same design family so a long table can stagger without looking mismatched.
Do you supply holders that suit a hotel's fire regulation?
We can specify a recessed candle seat and a non-combustible base for public spaces, and supply the specification with the order for your compliance file.
Is the crystal lead-free for a food and beverage venue?
We can select lead-free material grades for items that sit in a food or beverage environment and provide the relevant test reports for your destination market — tell us the application.
Can the holder be branded?
A small logo on the base or an etched ring around the column works for hotel and spa programmes without disturbing the candle.
How do you ship an item with a thin neck or a tall column?
Tall pieces are braced at the base and the neck separately inside a moulded box, then packed outer in a double-wall carton sized to the height.
