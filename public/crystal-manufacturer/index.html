Material and testing

Most pieces are cut from K9 optical crystal, the grade most buyers expect for awards, gifts and decorative work. Every piece we ship is lead-free, so the finished product stays safe for tableware, cosmetics and children’s gift lines.

Technical files are available on request: material specification, lead content, and the test reports that back them. We hold SGS reports, ISO 9001 certification and RoHS compliance documentation, and we can issue the same reports against your own order or under your company name where your market requires it.

If your buyer asks for a specific composition — a different refractive index, a coloured or gradient block, or a soda-lime glass alternative for a lower cost point — tell us at the quotation stage and we will confirm what the piece can be made from before you commit.

Shipping, payment and new tooling

We ship under FOB Ningbo or FOB Shanghai, and can quote CIF or DDP to your port when you prefer to compare landed cost.

Payment is a 30% deposit by T/T with the balance of 70% against a copy of the bill of lading, which keeps your deposit at risk only until the goods are loaded. For established accounts we can review other arrangements on a first order of reasonable size.

Where a shape warrants a new mould, we open it — for the products where tooling is needed the tooling cost is not billed separately, and we confirm that in writing before production starts. Standard engraving and finishing setups are handled on the same production line as the cast and polished body, so engraved orders run on the same schedule as plain ones.

Export packing is a five-layer carton as standard, with a gift box or extra safety packing used when the product or your retail channel calls for it. Damages in transit are assessed from photographs of the carton and the piece: send us images and we will come back with a replacement or a credit note.

Frequently asked questions about working with our factory

What is the minimum order quantity?
A single design starts at 60 to 100 pieces, and orders can be mixed across designs. From 500 pieces the unit price improves. The exact floor depends on the size of the product, so tell us the piece you want and we will confirm the count for it.
How long does production take?
Samples take 7 to 10 days from confirmed artwork. Bulk orders take 25 to 55 days depending on quantity and finish. We confirm a production date against your delivery deadline before you pay the deposit.
Do I pay for a sample, and is it refunded?
Each sample is costed from the piece and the engraving, so we quote it before making it. Once an order reaches USD 8,000, part of the sample fee is credited back to that order.
Is the crystal lead-free and which reports can you provide?
All pieces are made in lead-free K9 optical crystal. We hold SGS reports, ISO 9001 certification and RoHS compliance documents, and can issue reports for your order.
What payment and shipping terms do you use?
FOB Ningbo or Shanghai by default, with CIF and DDP available. Payment is 30% deposit by T/T and 70% against a copy of the bill of lading.
Can you open a new mould for a shape I design?
Where the shape can be produced with tooling, yes — and for the products where a mould is needed the tooling cost is not charged separately. Send the drawing and we will tell you which shapes are viable.

Why work directly with the manufacturer?
