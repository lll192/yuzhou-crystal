How crystal home decor and figurines are made

Every piece follows the same four-stage route, whether it is a single trophy or a full container of component parts. Knowing the sequence helps when you are setting a deadline.

Material selection

The run starts with the block. Clarity, inclusions and the optical tone are chosen before cutting, because the material decides how the facets will read once polished.

Cutting and shaping

The block is sawn, ground and rough-shaped to the silhouette on CNC and conventional equipment, then the facet pattern or the profile is cut into the body.

Polishing and finishing

Every face is polished in sequence — coarse through to final — which is what separates a mirror facet from a hazy one. Edges are beveled at this stage to stop chipping in service.

Engraving and packing

Laser, sandblast or etch work is applied to the approved artwork, the piece is inspected against the sample, then boxed individually for export.

What you can customise on decor pieces

The list below is what buyers most often specify. Anything on it is quoted per unit, so the clearer your brief at the start, the faster the quotation.

  • Form — an existing mould, a modified silhouette or a shape developed from your reference
  • Figurine detailing — internal facets, an inset element or a combined crystal-and-metal build
  • Size range — one design developed across three or four sizes for a tiered price range
  • Finish — mirror polish, full frosted, satin, coloured gradient or a coated surface
  • Colour and clarity — clear, tinted, smoked or a two-tone assembled body
  • Base and hardware — metal stand, wooden block, acrylic plinth or a plain sealed base
  • Retail packaging — gift box, sleeve, certificate card and a private-label barcode

Frequently asked questions about crystal home decor and figurines

Can one design be made in several sizes?
Yes. Scaling a single silhouette across sizes is the cheapest way to build a range, because the tooling and the engraving setup are shared. We usually recommend three sizes for a first range.
Do you make pieces from our own drawings or 3D files?
Yes. A 2D drawing, a CAD file or a physical reference is enough for us to quote. For a fully new shape we can take your sketch through to a mould.
How is a figurine secured so it does not tip over?
We specify a weighted base, a felt or rubber foot, or an internal metal pin, depending on the form. Stability is part of the spec we quote, not an afterthought.
Can the pieces be branded under our own label?
Yes — engraving on the base, a printed card, a bar-coded retail box or the full private-label packaging can all be done under your brand.
What is the lead time for a new decorative shape?
A development shape takes longer than a stock form because it involves a mould and a sample round. We will tell you the development and production stages in the quote so you can plan the season.
Are the pieces suitable for a gift shipper's drop test?
Our export cartons are braced for single-piece retail shipping. Tell us the destination and the courier and we will specify the box accordingly.

Still deciding? Send us the design, the quantity and the date you need it by, and we will come back with a quotation and a production plan.
