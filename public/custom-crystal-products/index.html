What you can develop with us

A custom programme usually starts from a photo, a sample in your hand or a drawing that is not quite finished. These are the shapes we take on most often.

  • Award shapes — cups, columns, shields, blocks and plates for competitions and anniversaries
  • Decorative pieces — figurines, architectural forms and seasonal sculptures for retail
  • Corporate gifts — paperweights, desk accessories and logo pieces for sales teams
  • Frame and display bodies — bevel-edged frames, domes and display stands
  • Fragrance components — bottle bodies, stoppers and pendant hardware
  • Table and bar pieces — tumblers, decanters and servingware in cut or plain finish
  • Vases and bowls — from a short table vase to a floorstanding piece
  • Private label sets — crystal plus retail box, insert and printed sheet under your brand

Materials, engraving and finishing

The crystal body, the marking method and the surface finish are three separate choices, and most projects use a combination of them.

  • Material — K9 optical crystal, lead-free as standard across every product we make
  • Surface engraving — laser or diamond marking for logos, names and serial lines
  • 3D inner engraving — a raised image cut inside the crystal body, best at 8 mm thickness or more
  • Artwork — vector files give the cleanest result; a 300 dpi bitmap is acceptable for most logos
  • Colour and plating — gold, silver and rose-gold plated inserts and coloured gradients on the edge
  • Base and fitting — a flat felt pad, a metal collar, a wooden disc or a weighted foot
  • Certification — SGS reports, ISO 9001 certification and RoHS documentation available on request
  • Factory scale — 3,500 m² of production floor, 65 people, around 50,000 pieces a month

Frequently asked questions about custom projects

Do I need a finished drawing?
No. A photo, a sample or a rough sketch is enough for the first pass. We tell you what has to change before it can be cut.
What is the smallest order you will take?
Around 60–100 pieces per design. You can mix several designs into one order to keep the total lower.
How long until I hold a sample?
7–10 days for a standard shape. Anything that needs a new tool waits for the tool first.
Is the sample fee credited back?
Part of it comes back once the production order passes USD 8,000. We confirm the amount in writing with the quote.
Do you really not charge tooling?
For products running on a standard tool there is no tooling charge. A genuinely new shape is quoted separately before you commit.
How quickly will I get a price?
A designer reviews every request and comes back with a feasibility note and an estimate. Send the brief and form above and we will reply within one business day.
