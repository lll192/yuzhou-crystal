How crystal office and desk gifts are made

Every piece follows the same four-stage route, whether it is a single trophy or a full container of component parts. Knowing the sequence helps when you are setting a deadline.

Material selection

The run starts with the block. Clarity, inclusions and the optical tone are chosen before cutting, because the material decides how the facets will read once polished.

Cutting and shaping

The block is sawn, ground and rough-shaped to the silhouette on CNC and conventional equipment, then the facet pattern or the profile is cut into the body.

Polishing and finishing

Every face is polished in sequence — coarse through to final — which is what separates a mirror facet from a hazy one. Edges are beveled at this stage to stop chipping in service.

Engraving and packing

Laser, sandblast or etch work is applied to the approved artwork, the piece is inspected against the sample, then boxed individually for export.

What you can customise on desk gifts

The list below is what buyers most often specify. Anything on it is quoted per unit, so the clearer your brief at the start, the faster the quotation.

  • Format — paperweight, block, sphere, column, or a functional piece with a desk base
  • Material and clarity — optical crystal, glass or a crystal-plus-metal combination
  • Logo placement — base edge, top face, rear panel or a fully wrapped engraving
  • Engraving method — laser, sandblast fill, etch or internal laser marking
  • Personalisation — name, role, date, department or a full campaign line
  • Functional add-ons — a metal pen slot, a coaster base or a combined cork and crystal foot
  • Packaging — presentation box, brand sleeve, certificate and a gift receipt option

Frequently asked questions about crystal office and desk gifts

What works better for a logo on a desk object?
A shallow engraving on an edge or a base reads as deliberate; a large flat logo on the top face tends to look like advertising. Our art team will place it for you and show a proof.
Can the same design be produced in three price tiers?
Yes — usually through size, material and packaging rather than three different shapes, which keeps the brand line visually consistent across a programme.
Do you handle personalisation for a large employee cohort?
Yes. We build a name list template, run the engraving line against it and deliver in the sequence or the split you ask for — by department, location or shipping address.
Is a crystal paperweight heavy enough to feel premium?
Weight is what separates a paperweight from a paperweight-shaped ornament, so we specify the thickness and the base for the hand feel rather than for the lightest possible piece.
Can we order for several offices in different countries?
Yes. We can ship to multiple addresses from one order, with each consignment packed under its own label and shipping mark.
What happens if an engraved piece arrives damaged?
We pack to a drop-tested spec and we replace any unit that breaks in transit — tell us the batch mark and we will restock it on the next run.

Still deciding? Send us the design, the quantity and the date you need it by, and we will come back with a quotation and a production plan.
