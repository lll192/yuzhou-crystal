What you find in each collection

Our catalogue is grouped into eight crystal collections. Each one covers a different use — awards and presentation pieces, interior decoration, corporate gifts, table settings or fragrance components. Every collection is produced from the same K9 optical crystal body, so finish, engraving and packaging options stay consistent from one group to the next. What changes is shape, tooling and the way a piece is packed for shipping.

  • Trophies and awards — cups, columns, shields, plates and custom award shapes, often with a coloured or mirrored base.
  • Home decor and figurines — decorative sculptures, animals, architectural forms and seasonal pieces for retail shelves.
  • Office and desk gifts — paperweights, desk accessories, keyrings and corporate logo pieces produced in small or large runs.
  • Photo frames — bevel-edged frames in portrait and landscape, with stand or wall fixing.
  • Candle holders — pressed and cut holders for tea lights, tapers and pillar candles.
  • Tableware and barware — tumblers, wine and whisky glasses, decanters and serving pieces.
  • Perfume and fashion components — bottle bodies, stoppers, pendants and small fashion hardware.
  • Vases — crystal vases and bowls in a range of heights, supplied with a separately supported base.

How our collections are made

Every piece follows the same four-stage route through the factory. Because cutting and engraving run on the same line, pieces with personalisation carry the same lead time as plain ones.

Material selection

Blank crystal is inspected before it is cut. We use K9 optical crystal, and every piece leaving the factory is lead-free.

Cutting and shaping

Shapes are cut to the tool drawing, then edges are bevelled and faceted on the grinding line.

Polishing and finishing

Each piece is polished to a clear or frosted surface. Colour, plating and inner 3D engraving are applied here.

Engraving and packing

Artwork is set from your vector file or a 300 dpi bitmap, then the finished piece goes into an export carton.

Commercial terms across the collections

These terms hold across all eight collections. Figures move with product size and finish, so confirm them against the piece you have in mind.

  • Minimum order — around 60–100 pieces per design, and several designs can be mixed into one order.
  • Lead time — samples in 7–10 days, bulk production in 25–55 days.
  • Sampling — the sample fee is charged by the piece, and part of it is refunded once the order reaches USD 8,000.
  • Tooling — tooling is not charged for the products that use a standard tool.
  • Material — K9 optical crystal, lead-free across the range, SGS reports available.
  • Export cartons — five-layer export carton, gift box or protective packing to your specification.
  • Trade terms — FOB Ningbo or Shanghai, T/T 30% deposit and 70% against bill of lading copy.
  • Markets — we ship to North America, Europe, Australia and South-East Asia.

Questions about the collections

Can I mix several designs in one first order?
Yes. The minimum of roughly 60–100 pieces applies per design, but you can combine designs and still ship as one order.
Do you charge for tooling?
For products made on an existing tool there is no tooling charge. Some new shapes need a new tool, and we quote that before you commit.
How long from asking to a sample?
Around 7–10 days for a standard piece. A shape that needs new tooling starts once the tool is ready.
Can I use my own artwork?
Yes. Vector files are the best result; a 300 dpi bitmap also engraves acceptably. We check the file before setting the plate.
Is the crystal lead-free?
All pieces are made in lead-free K9 optical crystal. SGS, ISO 9001 and RoHS documentation can be provided on request.
What happens if a shipment arrives damaged?
It depends on the product and how it arrived, so send us photos of the carton and the piece and we will tell you the right remedy.
