Where our crystal photo frames are used

A crystal photo frame is a gift that has to work in two settings — on a shelf as an object, and holding an actual photograph. We make commemorative frames for weddings, funerals, anniversaries, military and corporate presentation, in sizes matched to standard photo formats.

Weddings and anniversaries

A framed keepsake with names and a date, usually ordered as a set for the couple and their families.

Corporate presentation

Long-service and retirement frames handed over at an event, in a standard brand format.

Military and memorial

Formal presentation pieces where the engraving has to be exact and permanent.

Retail gifting

A frame sold with a photo mount insert, which makes it easy to gift with no extra step.

How crystal photo frames are made

Every piece follows the same four-stage route, whether it is a single trophy or a full container of component parts. Knowing the sequence helps when you are setting a deadline.

Material selection

The run starts with the block. Clarity, inclusions and the optical tone are chosen before cutting, because the material decides how the facets will read once polished.

Cutting and shaping

The block is sawn, ground and rough-shaped to the silhouette on CNC and conventional equipment, then the facet pattern or the profile is cut into the body.

Polishing and finishing

Every face is polished in sequence — coarse through to final — which is what separates a mirror facet from a hazy one. Edges are beveled at this stage to stop chipping in service.

Engraving and packing

Laser, sandblast or etch work is applied to the approved artwork, the piece is inspected against the sample, then boxed individually for export.

What you can customise on frames

The list below is what buyers most often specify. Anything on it is quoted per unit, so the clearer your brief at the start, the faster the quotation.

  • Photo size — 4x6, 5x7, 8x10 and larger, or a bespoke aperture for a non-standard print
  • Frame form — portrait, landscape, angled easel, hanging pane or a two-piece assembly
  • Material — clear optical crystal, frosted glass or a crystal and wood combination
  • Aperture finish — a bevel around the opening, a frosted inner border or a plain cut
  • Stand and display — fold-back easel, hanging fixings or a freestanding block base
  • Engraving — names, date, unit, rank, quote or a full presentation plate
  • Packaging — gift box with a photo mount, a card insert and a private-label sleeve

Frequently asked questions about crystal photo frames

Which photo sizes do you support?
Common apertures are 4x6, 5x7 and 8x10, plus larger bespoke sizes. If you use an unusual format we will size the aperture to your print rather than force it.
Does the photograph sit behind the crystal?
Usually yes — the print slips into a recess behind the crystal pane, which keeps it protected while remaining viewable. We will show you the assembly in the sample.
Can you engrave a full plaque with a long message?
Yes. Longer text moves to a separate brass or crystal plate below the photo, because a wide line of text inside the frame opening makes the aperture look cramped.
Do you make frames for funeral or memorial use?
Yes. We handle sensitive, exacting orders with permanent engraving and a plain presentation box, and we confirm the wording on a proof before production.
Can the frame stand on its own without hanging?
Most desktop frames use a fold-back easel or a weighted block base. Tell us where it will stand and we will specify the display that works there.
Will the crystal cloud over time?
A polished crystal stays clear indefinitely; the risk is edge chipping rather than fogging. We finish edges to a bevelled profile so daily handling does not dull them.

Still deciding? Send us the design, the quantity and the date you need it by, and we will come back with a quotation and a production plan.
